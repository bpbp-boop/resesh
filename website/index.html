questions

anything missing, open an issue on github.

is it actually free? yes. free during the beta and free after it. the source is on github.
does anything leave my machine? no. sessions, credentials and recordings stay in your local profile. there is no account and no telemetry.
where are credentials kept? in the windows credential store, encrypted per user. resesh never writes a password to its own config files.
which protocols are supported? ssh, telnet, serial and local shells today. rdp is in progress for a later beta.
mac or linux? not yet. windows is the focus while the beta stabilises.
can i script it? sessions and workspaces are plain files, so they diff and version-control cleanly. a cli for launching a workspace is planned.
